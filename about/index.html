What it is

piperpiperpiper is an engine: a behaviour-based, automated curator that helps raise awareness of the global job market and widen its reach.

It is AI-powered: the curation rules are written and kept up to date by AI, and then run on their own.

Good jobs are scattered across hundreds of company boards and feeds, and most never reach the people who would fit them. The engine gathers them in one place, once a day, with no people in the loop.

How it curates

It curates by behaviour, not by opinion. It reads what employers publish and keeps the jobs that behave well:

  • remote, with a clear statement of who may apply
  • fresh, and removed once they age out
  • open about pay, shown as a single monthly figure in US dollars, with the original kept
  • straight from the employer or an open board, never copied from a scraper

Every listing links back to where it came from, and the source is named on the page.

For people and for agents

People browse the jobs. Agents read the same data as /jobs.json and /llms.txt, so the reach is not limited to a web page.
